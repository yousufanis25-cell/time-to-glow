THE PERSON BEHIND THE IDEA

Hello, I'm Yousuf.

I believe learning should go beyond books and classrooms. It should be practical, interesting, creative and connected to real life.

Through Time To Glow, I want to share knowledge, teach useful skills, discuss meaningful ideas and encourage people to think independently. Whether it's understanding an Arduino circuit, learning a piano melody or exploring the universe, every subject has something fascinating to teach us.

My Mission

To make knowledge accessible, encourage curiosity, develop problem-solving abilities and inspire people to become lifelong learners.


My Belief

You don't have to know everything. You just have to stay curious enough to keep learning.
